# Screenshot plan

App Store Connect requires at least one Mac screenshot at:

- **2880×1800** (16:10) — preferred
- or 2560×1600

Submit 4–6 to fill the gallery. Each gets a one-line caption above it on the listing — write captions short.

Capture on your own Mac with `Cmd+Shift+4 → Spacebar → click window`, then resize/letterbox in Preview to 2880×1800 (or use Acorn / Sketch / Figma to drop the window screenshot onto a coloured 2880×1800 backdrop).

## Order + caption suggestions

1. **Hero — main window with Photo style wallpaper + glass card + AI blurb.**
   Caption: `A new photograph every day, with a short note about the place.`

2. **Menu bar popover open over a desktop with current wallpaper.**
   Caption: `One click from the menu bar — rotate, regenerate, pause.`

3. **Three widget sizes laid out together (small / medium / large) in the macOS widget gallery.**
   Caption: `Three widget styles, three sizes — captions tuned to each.`

4. **Settings → Connections tab showing Ollama + Unsplash setup with green status dots.**
   Caption: `Local AI by default. Your photos never leave your Mac.`

5. **Settings → Schedule tab showing rotation rule.**
   Caption: `Every hour, every morning, every Friday — you decide.`

6. **Library / Discover tab showing a grid of past wallpapers.**
   Caption: `Browse, favourite, and revisit anything you've seen.`

## App icon

App Store Connect pulls the icon directly from the app bundle. Make sure `Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/` has every required slot filled in 1× and 2× — particularly the 1024×1024 marketing icon (no transparency, no rounded corners; macOS rounds it for you).
